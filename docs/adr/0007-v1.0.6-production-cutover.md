# ADR 0007: v1.0.6 production cutover, closed beta and journey truth language

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-09-10

## Context

Production (`landingnl.com`) still served the recovered phone/SMS prototype: client-side
sessions, a displayed verification code, unprotected `/dashboard`, a paywall that granted
"Pro" without payment and simulated DigiD, bank and huisarts completion. The secure Worker
foundation (PR #4) was verified on staging only. The privacy P0 gates in
`docs/PRIVACY_GDPR_PLAN.md` (controller identity, processing record, processor terms,
retention, data-subject workflows, published notice) are not yet met.

## Decision

1. Replace the prototype in production with the PR #4 Worker foundation plus the fixes below.
2. Owner decision (2026-09-10): production Google sign-in is **open to anyone**
   (`SIGNUP_MODE=open`) and ships together with a published Privacy Notice (`/privacy`, v1.0)
   linked from the welcome, login, onboarding and dashboard pages. The signup gate stays in
   the code: setting `SIGNUP_MODE=closed_beta` admits only D1 `beta_invites` (migration 0003)
   or `BETA_ALLOWLIST` addresses, and the check runs before any user row is written.
   Remaining P0 items (processing record, processor terms on file, retention job, self-service
   export/deletion) are tracked in `docs/PRIVACY_GDPR_PLAN.md`; until then deletion requests
   are handled manually by email within 30 days, as stated in the notice. The check runs before any user row is
   written, so rejected visitors leave no personal data. `SIGNUP_MODE=open` is set only after
   the privacy P0 gates pass.
3. Pull forward the *claim-removal* part of PR J3 (not the rule registry): journey steps are
   user-confirmed milestones labelled "(beyan)", progress counts only those milestones, the
   registration appointment date no longer completes the BSN step, no bank is shown as
   active or integrated, huisarts guidance tells students to confirm capacity directly, and the
   exchange board is labelled a private preview with sample posts marked as samples.
4. The journey branches on citizenship status. Phase 1 opens with a status route card:
   non-EU/EEA students see the IND route (MVV where applicable, VVR before municipal
   registration, employer-side TWV), EU/EEA/Swiss students see that no permit is required and
   start at BRP/RNI registration. Step guidance for municipal registration, bank account and
   completion differs accordingly. The four Settle milestones stay common to both routes.
5. Minimum onboarding age is 16 (UAVG art. 5 age of digital consent); no birth date is prefilled.
6. Email sign-in is hidden and refused until Brevo is configured, is subject to the signup gate,
   and is limited to 3 unexpired links per address per 15 minutes; no token is written on refusal.
7. Unknown or revoked session cookies (including the prototype's `landingnl_session=active`)
   are cleared instead of producing a 404 on `/`.
8. `robots.txt` and `favicon.ico` are served without a database call.
9. The top-level Wrangler config is renamed `landingnl-local` so a bare `wrangler deploy` cannot
   overwrite production; production disables `workers.dev`.
10. Pushes to `release/**` deploy staging; pushes to `main` deploy production with migration,
   smoke and prototype-removal checks.

## Consequences

- Production stops exposing the insecure prototype; personal data is collected only after the Privacy Notice is shown.
- The page-hash baselines are intentionally re-approved for v1.0.6.
- J2–J6 remain separate; the rule registry (`regulatory_rules`) is still empty.

## Rollback

`wrangler rollback --env production` restores the previous Worker version. D1 schema
(migrations 0001–0003) is additive and does not need rollback.
